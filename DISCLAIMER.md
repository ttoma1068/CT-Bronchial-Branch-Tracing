# Research and clinical disclaimer

BBT is a research prototype intended for research, education and pre-procedural planning.

It is not established by this repository as a clinically validated navigation system and does not determine the real-time position or orientation of a bronchoscope.

Procedural anatomy may differ from the pre-procedural CT because of respiration, patient position, sedation or anaesthesia, atelectasis, airway deformation, secretions, segmentation error, incomplete distal-airway visibility and other CT-to-body divergence.

Users must check outputs against the source CT and comply with applicable research, information-governance, regulatory and clinical requirements.

No generated airway should be treated as anatomically real unless supported by the CT or by a documented clinician correction based on the CT.
